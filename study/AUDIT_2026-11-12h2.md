# AUDIT h2: the study's intervals with independent draws per task (2026-11-12)

Scripts: `results/audit_2026-11-12h2/`. `run_all.sh` reruns everything: one driver per run, then `h2_table.py`, which writes `h2_table.{json,txt,md}`.

## 1. Method

- **The job's own code.** Each driver extracts `infra/` at the run's commit with `git archive` and imports the job's module. It rebuilds the job's inputs from the stored rows: items in the job's order, and every condition's rows, including rows the job read from earlier runs. It then calls the job's own result functions and sentence rules: `tables`, `summarize`, `diff_pair`, `ratio_summary`, `srows_summary`, `dist_readings`, `pair_readings`, `localize`, `controls`, `d4_sentences`, `room_word` and the others.
- **Draw schemes.** `h2lib.py` swaps the module's `task_draws` for each scheme:
  - the job's own, seeded `[SEED, byte sum of the task name]`, in which ins_word and sub_word share 869 and ins_char and sub_char share 839;
  - independent per task, seeded `[SEED, *t.encode()]`, at the job's 2,000 draws and at 10,000;
  - 50 more seeds, `[SEED + s, *t.encode()]` for s = 1 to 50, at 2,000 draws.
- **Every stored interval reproduces exactly.** With the job's draws, every interval and every string the drivers rebuild equals the stored metrics. That is 7,141 interval paths and 408 string paths over the 17 jobs, with a largest end difference of 0.0.
  - `select_1`'s stored values are `results/select_1/combined_metrics.json`, since its combine step wrote no S3 object.
  - Extra inputs, each copied read-only and ETag-checked:
    - `diff_1` used `map_1`'s H-alone rows. Its gate 27 required them equal.
    - `join_1` used F's flags from `explore/explore_pre_3/F_dev/items.parquet`.
- **Scope.** Every phase-2 run named in the request has a colliding pair with equal counts, so none is skipped. All runs on the word or edit tasks have 40, 50 or 20 items per task. `join_1`, `join_2`, `join_3`, `join_4` and `pair_1` also have the character tasks. `chain_2` has ins_char and sub_char, 40 each.
- **The tag rules.**
  - An interval is "moved" when an end moves by more than 0.01 between the job's interval and the independent one at 10,000 draws, or when an end changes side of 0. Ends are rounded to 9 decimals first.
  - A row is "sentence changes" when the most common frozen sentence over the 50 seeds differs from the printed one and holds more than 25 of 50.
  - `*` marks a job end outside the range of the 50 independent seeds. Such an end lies where independent draws do not reach, which points to the seeding defect rather than draw-to-draw noise.
