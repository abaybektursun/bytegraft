## 2. Leakage (question 2)

- **The lists.** The fit, check, select, test and fresh lists are identical in the search session and the read session. Test equals `join_3`'s 160 edit ids and fresh equals `pair_1`'s 80.
- **Fit items against the other lists.** The 80 fit items share no id, no prompt last line and no gold with select, test or fresh.
  - They share no word or sentence with test or fresh.
  - They share 54 words or sentences with select (and 22 with check), each under another task. The select read is therefore not fully independent of the fit items; the held-out reads are.
- **The few-shot lines.** Each task has one block, the same in every item of the task. No item's word or sentence, and no item's gold with its quotes, appears in any block.
- **What F and S read during a decode.** The decode loop is unchanged since `7499bcaa4`, where AUDIT i §3 read it:
  - the donors start from the item's prompt alone (`start`, `_decide` at answer offset 0);
  - at each step they re-parse the prompt plus the bytes the host has written so far (`extend`, a forward over the whole text);
  - for Pythia the host's ids are id 0 followed by Pythia's tokens of the prompt, and the decode asserts that those tokens give back the prompt's bytes.
  - F and S never read the gold. The gold's byte length sets where every decode stops.
