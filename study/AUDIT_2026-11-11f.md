## 7. Advice (not an audit result)

The smallest follow-up that separates "agents add something" from "the table adds something" is one more arm at 60 scored sets with the
same table and no LLM. A scripted search would:
1. restrict candidates to the top 32 heads by a fixed table rule (direct effect or attention shift);
2. score 20 sets of 16 drawn from them;
3. fit the same additive ridge model the agents fitted;
4. take two more rounds of 20 from its top coefficients and single swaps.

Add CMA-ES or TPE over head membership, as research pass 9 named, without the table. Score both on fresh items (`pair_1`'s 80) beside
both agents and the gradient top 16.

How to read the result:
- If the scripted search is level with the agents, the finding is "the table plus a surrogate".
- If it is below them, something in the agents' reading of the table is doing work.

Published work answers part of this. MAIA (C3) beat a gradient-fitted selection (L1) at equal units; here the agents only tie the
gradient ranking. 2603.24647 (C5) found a classical optimizer ahead of pure LLM agents and a hybrid ahead of both, at equal evaluations;
our black-box arms were not that optimizer. No published test isolates "the agent" from "the evidence it is given".
